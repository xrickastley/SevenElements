Swirl ​

Swirl is the Elemental Reaction triggered when Anemo is applied onto an entity already affected by Pyro/Electro/Hydro/Cryo.

This reaction applies the involved non-Anemo element to all entities within a 5m radius excluding the entity the reaction was triggered on. Pyro, Electro and Cryo Swirl will also deal AoE DMG of the involved element, including the entity the reaction was triggered on, while Hydro Swirl only spreads the Hydro aura and deals DMG to the target that the reaction was triggered upon.

Internal Data ​

Reaction Multiplier: 0.6
Reaction ID(s):

  • seven-elements:swirl_pyro (triggered on the Pyro aura)
  • seven-elements:swirl_hydro (triggered on the Hydro aura)
  • seven-elements:swirl_electro (triggered on the Electro aura)
  • seven-elements:swirl_cryo (triggered on the Cryo aura)
  • seven-elements:swirl_frozen (triggered on the Freeze aura)
