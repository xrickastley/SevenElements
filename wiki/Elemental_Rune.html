Elemental Rune ​

An elemental rune is a crafting ingredient mainly used as an intermediate step in crafting element-related items. It is required to craft the infusion table and to duplicate elemental attunement smithing templates.

Obtaining ​

Crafting ​

In Survival mode, elemental runes are obtainable only through crafting.

2

Usage ​

The only usage of elemental runes is to craft an infusion table, duplicate elemental attunement smithing templates or be used as a smithing ingredient for elemental attunement.
